Dear St. Vrain Valley Community,
Welcome to the 2026-2027 school year. There is real joy in these first weeks of August, when our buildings fill again with students in the hallways and teachers greeting familiar faces and new ones at classroom doors. St. Vrain maintains among the highest base teacher pay in Colorado, and we have been recognized by Forbes as a Best-In-State Employer again for the second year, which helps us bring exceptional educators into our classrooms and keep them here.

Teach to Reach

We are grateful for your support, which allows us to provide our students with world-class facilities.
I have spent my entire career in public education because of what a public school system can be for the community around it. We open our doors to every child who lives here, whatever their circumstances or their ambitions, and we build around each of them the programs, relationships and support they need to thrive. That is the
Public School Advantage

This fall, I will again host open community meetings in several of our geographic areas, and I hope you will join me. The conversations I had with families last year stayed with me, and I want to hear from you again about what is working in our schools and where we can better serve you.
To our families, thank you for trusting us with your children. To our teachers and staff, thank you for the care and skill you bring to this work every day. Together, we will make this a joyful and successful year for every student in our St. Vrain schools.
Sincerely,
Jackie Kapushion, Ed.D.

### Reminder: Free or reduced benefit applications available

- Published: 2026-08-24T14:12:06-06:00
- Source URL: https://www.svvsd.org/2026/08/24/reminder-free-reduced-price-meal-applications-available-2/

Families who believe they may qualify for free or reduced eligibility benefits are encouraged to complete an application by Oct. 1 if they have not already done so. While all St. Vrain Valley students in preschool through grade twelve receive one free breakfast and lunch each day at participating schools through Colorado’s Healthy School Meals for All program, completing the application can provide additional benefits for families and schools.
Families who qualify receive valuable savings such as waived or reduced school fees, discounts on college entrance exams and applications, reduced rates for internet and utilities, and access to the Summer-EBT program.
When more households apply, St. Vrain schools receive increased federal funding that supports classrooms, after-school programs, preschool opportunities, technology, and additional snacks and meals outside regular school hours. While the Oct. 1 deadline offers additional benefits, free or reduced-price meal applications are continuously accepted and processed throughout the school year, and families are invited to apply or reapply at any time. They can be
completed online
, or paper copies are available at Nutrition Services, 2929 Clover Basin Dr., Longmont, CO 80503. The application is completely confidential and not shared with any other agency or organization. To learn more, visit the
Nutrition Services webpage
.

### Niwot soccer standout Cora Roberts trains with USA Deaf Youth National Team

- Published: 2026-08-19T12:14:00-06:00
- Source URL: https://www.svvsd.org/2026/08/19/niwot-soccer-standout-cora-roberts-trains-with-usa-deaf-youth-national-team/

Every time Cora Roberts takes to the pitch with Niwot soccer, she stands out among the rest of her team. The 16-year-old junior has hearing loss in both ears, meaning she often has to communicate with her hands instead of her voice.
Her coaches throughout the years have always made the necessary adjustments, with some going as far as to learn sign language to supplement what she can’t pick up through her hearing aids, but none have provided an experience quite like the one she had with the USA Deaf Soccer Youth National Team this summer.
For a few weeks, Roberts had the chance to practice and scrimmage with teenagers from all over the country, all of whom have some level of hearing loss. The experience, her mother Kelsey said, was eye-opening.

### Silver Creek High School Named Disney on Broadway School of Excellence

- Published: 2026-08-19T09:49:13-06:00
- Source URL: https://www.svvsd.org/2026/08/19/silver-creek-high-school-named-disney-on-broadway-school-of-excellence/

Silver Creek High School has been named a Disney on Broadway School of Excellence, recognizing the school’s exceptional student-led involvement in theatre production and its commitment to creativity, leadership, and student engagement.
Created in 2024 to celebrate 30 years of Disney on Broadway, the School of Excellence program recognizes middle and high schools across the United States that demonstrate outstanding student involvement in theatrical disciplines, including stagecraft, direction, performance, choreography and design.
As part of the recognition, winning schools receive free licensing and production materials to produce a Disney musical during the 2026-2027 school year, along with exclusive educational webinars featuring industry professionals. Silver Creek will celebrate this honor by performing
Disney’s High School Musical
on November 12, 13, and 14 along with two other unique productions throughout the year.
“This opportunity is especially exciting because it reflects how much our theatre program has grown, not only in the number of students involved, but in the number of students stepping forward as leaders,” said Tracy Knick, the Silver Creek Theater Director. “Bringing
Disney’s High School Musical
to Silver Creek is a wonderful way to celebrate that growth while giving even more students the opportunity to discover their voice, talents, and potential as leaders.”
Through its upcoming productions, Silver Creek students will have opportunities to strengthen their skills, collaborate across theatrical disciplines and take greater ownership of the creative process. The school’s recognition as a Disney on Broadway School of Excellence provides an opportunity to celebrate the program’s growth while inviting even more students and community members to experience the power of theatre.

### Westview Middle School Named 2026 AMLE School of Distinction

- Published: 2026-08-17T12:36:39-06:00
- Source URL: https://www.svvsd.org/2026/08/17/westview-middle-school-named-2026-amle-school-of-distinction/

Westview Middle School has been named a 2026 School of Distinction by The Association for Middle Level Education (AMLE), recognizing the school’s commitment to creating a student-centered environment grounded in best practices for middle grades education. They are the first and only Colorado middle school to be honored with this distinction.
AMLE’s Schools of Distinction program recognizes schools serving students ages 10-15 for their commitment to the middle grades best practices outlined in
The Successful Middle School: This We Believe
. The program also guides schools through a process of self-assessment and strategic vision setting to support continuous improvement.
“Westview and our entire community believe in doing what is best for students above all else. To receive this prestigious, national recognition from AMLE is truly an honor and a strong validation of the students, staff, and community that make our work possible,” shared DJ Eipper, Westview Middle School Assistant Principal. “At its core, Westview is truly a student-centered school where every decision is grounded in what is best for students academically, socially, and emotionally. The school has created a culture in which students are not only expected to achieve at high levels but are also provided with the support, opportunities, and encouragement needed to do so.”
The recognition adds to Westview’s strong record of excellence in middle level education. The school is also a four-time Schools to Watch recipient from the Colorado Association of Middle Level Education (CAMLE).
Westview is proud to continue implementing middle level practices that help students develop as strong, caring, kind and intelligent learners while preparing them for success in high school and beyond.
